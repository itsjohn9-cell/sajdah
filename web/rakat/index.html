Keep this page open with the screen on. For flat-mat placement, put the phone beside the prostration area with the front camera facing up, never under your forehead. Check that standing, sitting and prostration produce different views. A propped view works too. The camera is required; optional sensors assist but cannot confirm unseen prayer movements.

Fast movements are supported with short recognition windows: about 200 ms for raised postures and 400 ms for sajdah. Shorter or obscured movements may be missed. These are noise filters, not instructions about prayer timing. Unknown frames never count as a release.

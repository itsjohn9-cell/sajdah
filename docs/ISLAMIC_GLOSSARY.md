# Islamic Terminology & Fiqh Reference Guide

## Canonical Authority
All Islamic terms, transliterations, spellings, and prayer movement definitions in **Sajdah** are standardized against the authoritative English edition of:

> **Ayatullah al-Uzma Sayyid Ali al-Husayni al-Sistani's *Tawdih al-Masa'il* (*Islamic Laws*)**  
> Official source: [sistani.org - Islamic Laws](https://www.sistani.org/english/book/48/)

Whenever any new Islamic term, movement, dhikr, prayer, or fiqh concept is introduced to this application, it **must** strictly conform to this reference.

---

## 1. Prayer Movements & Postures (*Af'al al-Salat*)

The core Namaz / prostration flow follows the obligatory sequence established in *Islamic Laws*:

| Posture / Movement | Canonical Spelling | Arabic | Islamic Laws Citation | Meaning / Definition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Standing** | **Qiyam** | قِيَام | *Ruling 958* | Standing upright while reciting Takbiratul Ihram and recitation (*Qira'ah*). |
| **2. Bowing** | **Ruku'** | رُكُوع | *Ruling 1022* | Bending down until hands reach the knees. Spelled with apostrophe for *'ayn*. |
| **3. Standing after Bowing** | **Qaumah** | قَوْمَة | *Ruling 1043* | Returning to a fully erect standing position before prostration. |
| **4. First Prostration** | **Sajdah 1** | سَجْدَة | *Ruling 1045* | Placing seven parts of the body on the ground (forehead on earth/turbah). |
| **5. Sitting between Sajdahs** | **Jalsah** | جَلْسَة | *Ruling 1074* | Sitting upright and stable between the first and second sajdah. |
| **6. Second Prostration** | **Sajdah 2** | سَجْدَة | *Ruling 1045* | The second prostration completing the rak'ah. |
| **7. Sitting for Testimony** | **Tashahhud** | تَشَهُّد | *Ruling 1100* | Recited sitting in the 2nd and final rak'ah. |
| **8. Concluding Salutations**| **Salam** | سَلَام | *Ruling 1105* | Concluding recitation of the prayer. |

### Units of Prayer
- **Rak'ah** (singular) — e.g., *"one rak'ah"*.
- **Rak'ahs** or **Rak'ats** (plural) — e.g., *"2 rak'ahs / rak'ats"*.
- **Sujud** — the general state/act of prostration; **Sajdah** — an individual act of prostration; **Sajdatayn** — the two sajdahs of a rak'ah.

## 3. Missed Obligations (*Qada / Qaza*)

- **Qada** (Arabic: قَضَاء) is the canonical transliteration used in Sistani's *Islamic Laws* (*Ruling 1370+*, chapter *"Qada Prayers"*).
- In Persian and Urdu literature, the letter *dād* (ض) is pronounced as *z*, hence **Qaza**.
- In this application, both forms are acknowledged: **"Qada (Qaza)"** so users from all linguistic backgrounds have full clarity while maintaining canonical adherence.

## 4. The Tasbih of Sayyidah Fatimah al-Zahra (sa)

Cited directly from *Islamic Laws*, **Ruling 1122**:

> *"After the prayer, it is recommended to recite the Tasbih of Lady Fatimah (sa):  
> 1. 34 times: **Allahu Akbar** (الله أكبر)  
> 2. 33 times: **Alhamdulillah** (الحمد لله)  
> 3. 33 times: **Subhanallah** (سبحان الله)  
> Total: 100."*

### Dhikr Transliterations
- **Allahu Akbar** — *"God is Greater"*
- **Alhamdulillah** — *"All praise is due to God"*
- **Subhanallah** — *"Glory be to God"*
- General remembrance: **Dhikr** (plural: **Adhkar**)

Sijda Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 October 2026

Sijda is an experimental Islamic companion app published by MJ. (Android package app.sijda.sijda). This policy covers the app and its web version, including optional camera tracking and tester feedback.

Contact and account / data deletion requests

For privacy questions, support, or data deletion requests, email fehmatics@gmail.com. Use the subject "Sajdah privacy" or "Sajdah data deletion". If requesting deletion of uploaded feedback or associated records, include your report ID shown after upload or your request details. Do not send prayer records, images, passwords, or other sensitive information.

Direct link to submit a data deletion request: Click here to send a pre-filled Data Deletion Request email to fehmatics@gmail.com. Requests are processed manually within 30 days, completely removing all matching server feedback records, associated anonymous identifiers, and any analysis exports controlled by the publisher.

Data stored on your device

Tasbeeh counts and settings, qaza prayer and fast balances, and saved tester feedback are stored locally in app/browser storage. Automatic camera sessions are temporary and are not saved as prayer history. Sijda does not upload your tasbeeh counts or qaza balances. These records may reveal religious practices; protect access to your device.

You can remove local records by clearing Sijda's app storage or the web version's site data. Uninstalling normally removes app-local data, but platform-managed backups or restoration may apply. Sijda has no custom cloud backup or cross-device synchronization for these records.

Camera and screen-light assistance

The camera starts only when you choose to enable it and grant permission. Frames and pose landmarks are processed on-device to estimate postures, rakat and sajdahs. Sijda does not record, save or upload camera images, video or raw body landmarks. The microphone is not requested.

The low-light check samples a small preview on-device; those samples and lighting measurements are not stored or uploaded. Optional screen light changes the display appearance and, on Android, temporarily raises app-window brightness. It does not change system brightness settings and is restored when tracking stops or the app leaves the foreground. Camera access can also be revoked through your device/browser settings.

Optional feedback uploads

Feedback is saved locally first. It is sent to our Firebase testing backend only if you select the upload-consent checkbox and submit. Not consenting does not prevent use of the counters or camera tracker. A labeled sample report is an example only and is never uploaded.

An uploaded report includes a report ID, session ID, creation time, algorithm version, session target, estimated and user-reported rakat/sajdah counts, correct/incorrect/unsure verdict, interruptions and completion status, phone placement, approximate distance, lighting category, device/browser description, optional notes, and up to 500 coarse posture-transition entries with relative times. These reports can reveal religious activity. Do not put names, contact information or other sensitive details in notes.

Firebase Authentication creates an anonymous identifier to authorize the upload; that identifier is stored with the report. You do not register an email/password account. Firebase receives network information such as your IP address when handling requests and may process service/security logs under its own terms. Sijda does not add your IP address as a feedback field.

Purpose, providers and location

MJ. uses feedback to investigate counting errors, compare placement and lighting conditions, and improve the app. Feedback does not automatically retrain a model or change your counters. Google Firebase provides authentication and database infrastructure. The feedback database is in Iowa, United States (us-central1); provider operations may involve processing in other countries.

Client access rules allow authorized report creation and deny client reads, lists, updates and deletes. The project owner can access reports through administrative tools and create private analysis exports. Data is transmitted over HTTPS. No security measure is an absolute guarantee.

See Google's privacy policy and Firebase privacy information. Web hosting and initial web-rendering asset delivery may also process ordinary network requests. This policy does not cover third-party sites you visit.

Retention and your choices

Uploaded feedback and associated anonymous authentication records have no automatic expiry and are retained until manually deleted by the project owner. Uninstalling or clearing local data does not delete uploaded reports.

Request deletion at fehmatics@gmail.com, preferably with the report ID. MJ. will use it to locate the report and associated identifier and remove matching owner-controlled records and analysis exports. If you have lost the ID, contact us; we may need limited information to identify your report and cannot guarantee locating it. Provider-managed logs or backups may follow the provider's retention processes. You can stop future uploads by leaving consent unchecked.

There is no in-app account-management or server-deletion button. Anonymous identifiers are created for feedback authorization, not a user-facing account with a login profile.

Advertising, analytics and children

Sijda does not sell personal data and has no advertising SDK, behavioral advertising, or app analytics SDK. It does not request location or contacts. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children. Do not include a child's personal information in tester feedback; contact us if such information has been submitted so it can be investigated and removed.

Changes

We will update this page and its effective date when practices change. Camera-based counts remain experimental estimates, not a determination of prayer validity.
